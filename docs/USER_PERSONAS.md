# User Personas — TransitIQ TMS

**Target Audience:** Enterprise Logistics Teams, Fleet Operations, Supply Chain Directors, and 3PL Providers in India.

## Persona 1: Rajesh Verma — VP of Supply Chain & Organisation Admin
- **Role:** Organisation Admin  
- **Email:** `admin@apexdist.in`  
- **Organization:** Apex Distribution India Pvt Ltd  
- **Location:** Gurgaon Corporate HQ  
- **Profile:** 20+ years leading pan-India supply chain networks for manufacturing conglomerates.
- **Goals:**
  - Maintain overall freight operating costs under ₹45/km across national corridors.
  - Ensure 95%+ On-Time In-Full (OTIF) delivery compliance for tier-1 automotive and retail clients.
  - Enforce financial governance on carrier rate revisions and emergency transshipment costs.
- **Pain Points:**
  - Fragmented visibility across regional 3PL partners.
