# Go-to-Market (GTM) Strategy — TransitIQ TMS

**Target Market:** Indian Enterprise Logistics, 3PLs, and Manufacturing Shippers  
**Initial Beachhead Segment:** Automotive Component Manufacturers & Consumer Goods Distributors in NCR, Maharashtra, and Tamil Nadu.

---

## 1. Pilot Program Strategy

### 1.1 The 90-Day Enterprise Pilot
- **Cohort Size:** 5 Mid-market Indian 3PLs or manufacturing shippers managing 50-200 daily linehaul movements.
- **Pilot Scope:** Focus on 3 high-density freight corridors:
  1. Gurgaon — Mumbai (NH-48 Western Corridor)
  2. Bengaluru — Chennai (NH-44 Southern Corridor)
  3. Delhi — Ahmedabad (Industrial Freight Belt)
- **Success Criteria:**
  - $> 12\%$ reduction in freight invoice dispute cycles.
  - $> 2.5$ hours faster incident turnaround through AI exception recommendations.
  - $> 8\%$ improvement in vehicle volumetric capacity fill rates.

---

## 2. Commercial Pricing Model

TransitIQ utilizes an operational tiered SaaS subscription based on monthly shipment volume:

| Tier | Monthly Shipments | Pricing (INR) | Features |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Growth 3PL** | Up to 500 loads/mo | ₹35,000 / month | Control tower, order intake, carrier procurement |
| **Enterprise Fleet** | Up to 2,500 loads/mo | ₹95,000 / month | AI Exception Manager, 3-way freight audit, Copilot |
| **National Scale** | Unlimited loads | Custom contract | Dedicated PostgreSQL instance, custom ERP integration |
